## 0. Intent
- **Claim** — The public MCP tells an AI assistant about 58 hand-written features (file last edited 2026-06-19) and 20 generic industry pages, while the product has 410 registered screens, 52 industry verticals and 28 website country markets. Assistants cannot find or correctly describe HelloGrowthCRM for most of what it does, and part of what it says is wrong (INR 1,099, "Unlimited users", Free tasks 500, USD $13).
- **Mechanism** — Serve a **verified capability catalogue and per-vertical module lists in which every record is published only after a recorded founder decision** (one shared, default-closed rule for both tool families), plus country-aware pricing wording reconciled across both pricing tools; add a **check that runs against the complete served responses** so the new content cannot regress into banned or structurally unsafe claims. Four draft PRs implement a first slice (#94 Free tasks value, #95 catalogue, #96 industry tools, #97 market scope); this plan governs them and **requires #95 and #96 to become default-closed and #97 to carry the reconciliation matrix** (§0a); they stay draft until rebuilt to this plan.
- **Observable** — `features_list` returns the original 58 entries plus only the catalogue entries the founder approved, and `industries_list_modules` / `industries_get_modules` return only the verticals the founder approved; each served record says when it was verified; legacy package flags and CRM-plan entitlement are separate, separately named fields; `pricing_get_plans` and `pricing_get_country_plans` agree with each other and state that pricing is country-aware; no entry presents a gated, off-by-default, industry-only, plan-unconfirmed or planned-only capability as generally available.
- **Risk** — A public machine-readable surface is repeated verbatim by AI assistants. Over-claiming (a feature that is super-admin-only, industry-only, TEST-only or not built) is worse than silence. Mitigations: default-closed publication, `availability_note` + `limitations` on every entry, structural and banned-claim checks on the served output, planned-not-built items excluded, founder decisions before any entry is served.

## 0c. The decision record (one schema for every catalogue entry and every vertical)
| Field | Meaning | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| `decision` | `pending` (default) · `approved` · `on_request` · `excluded` | `pending` and `excluded` are never served |
| `approver` | the person who decided | non-empty when not `pending` |
| `decided_on` | date of the decision | valid ISO date, not in the future |
| `verified_at` | date a person re-checked the capability against the product | valid ISO date, not in the future; **this** date drives freshness (a decision date is not a verification date) |
| `product_revision` | hellocrm commit the capability was verified against | non-empty when not `pending` |
| `enablement_scope` | `prod` · `master-only` · `industry:<key>` · `flag:<name>` | `master-only` is refused when `approved` (it may only be `excluded` or `pending`) |
| `crm_entitlement` | `{free, growth, enterprise}` each `true`/`false`/`"on_request"`/`null` | `null` = unknown; an `approved` entry may keep `null` only with `public_wording_ok` true |
| `public_wording_ok` | the founder accepted the served wording | boolean, required |
| `revoked_on` | date the decision was withdrawn | if set, the record is not served |
| invalidation | a product change to the capability's flag, plan gate or migration, or a CRM revision that removes it, makes the record stale | recorded in the CHANGELOG process; surfaced by the age rule |
Internal evidence (the product repo paths each claim was read from, and whether each exists on `app`) is **not** in the served data and not in this possibly-public repo; it lives in the founder-held workbook (*Verified_Capabilities*). Only an allowlisted public projection is served.

## 5. Blast radius
- **Backward** — AI clients calling `features_list` / `features_get`: the `category` enum gains eight values (old values still valid — **a consumer that exhaustively switches on the old enum could break; recorded in the CHANGELOG, AC-14**); responses gain `catalogue`, `availability_note`, `limitations`, `verified_at`, `entitlement_unconfirmed`. **`availability` stays present on every entry and keeps its legacy meaning — managed-service packages (Software Only/Starter, Growth Engine, RevOps Partner), now labelled `availability_domain: "managed_service_package"`.** For catalogue entries it is `{free:null, t1:null, t2:null, t3:null}` (unknown). CRM-plan entitlement (Free/Growth/Enterprise) is a **separate field, `crm_entitlement`**, never mapped onto the legacy tiers.
- **Filter contract** — the legacy `plan` filter is unchanged: it filters the package-domain flags and therefore returns legacy entries only; its description now says so. The new `crm_plan` filter (`free|growth|enterprise|all`) filters `crm_entitlement`: it returns entries where that plan is `true` or `"on_request"` in `features`, and lists served catalogue entries whose entitlement for that plan is `null` in `entitlement_unconfirmed` with a count, so no client is silently handed an incomplete catalogue and none is told an unconfirmed feature is on Free.
- **Forward** — the server reads two more static data files (no network, database or new dependency).
- **Sideways** — website pages and chatbot sources untouched; vertical MCP repos untouched. `/.well-known/mcp.json` and `/version` compute tool counts from the registry, so they move from 76 to 78 anonymous tools on their own.

## 6. Acceptance criteria
- **AC-01** — A `features_list` (category all) on the candidate returns the original 58 entries plus exactly the catalogue entries whose record is `approved` or `on_request` and not revoked; every served catalogue entry has `availability_note`, a non-empty `limitations` array and `verified_at`; the response has a `catalogue` stamp.
- **AC-02** — NEGATIVE: `check:served`, run over the **complete served responses** of every path in §0a, finds no `Unlimited users`, `SOC 2`, `WhatsApp-first`, `WhatsApp-native` or `₹1,099` **except the known-exceptions list**, where each existing violation names its owner and the prerequisite that removes it (₹1,099 → #91/#50; "Unlimited users" → #57; SOC 2 → Q6) and a stale exception fails. The criterion claims only what the scan proves; it does not claim absence of other over-claims (§8).
- **AC-03** — REGRESSION: the **integrated candidate stack** (#91 + #94 + #95 + #96 + #97 as one head, compared with `origin/main`, not with each PR's parent) differs from `origin/main` only by the entries of `src/data/served-baseline.json`: the two added tools; new properties; the eight added `category` values; the `crm_plan` filter; `availability_domain`; `market_scope`; the reworded global `best_for`; the task-boards Free allowance correction (500 → 100, a content correction); and the approved records. The original 58 entries are byte-identical in slug, title, summary, bullets and `availability` **except** that listed correction.
- **AC-04** — `industries_list_modules` returns only approved verticals; with every vertical approved it returns 52 (28 dedicated, 16 alias, 8 configuration-only), assigned screens total 317 plus 5 cross-cutting, which with the 88 core views account for all 410 registered views; with none approved it returns 0 verticals and a `pending_count` of 52.
- **AC-05** — Pricing reconciliation matrix. For each of the 8 markets (India, USA, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, Singapore, New Zealand) and each of {Free, Growth, Enterprise} × {monthly, annual} the test compares `pricing_get_plans` (india_inr and global_usd), `pricing_get_country_plans` and `market_scope` on **currency, amount, unit (per user per month), billing period and tax treatment** (INR: GST 18% additional; every other market: "tax treatment not stated" unless a value is decided). Wherever two tools state the same fact they must agree; the global USD Growth monthly equals the USA country row equals the USD number in `market_scope` (USD 12 per user per month); the India Growth number equals the India country row and `market_scope.india` (INR 899). **Founder-unconfirmed cells (USD annual; local-currency figures from the 2026-08-06 mirror) are reported as `UNCONFIRMED` in the test output and carry a known-exception tied to Q2; this plan does not certify them.** "A yearly discount exists" is asserted only where the served annual per-user rate is below the monthly rate in that market's row, and for India as "two months free"; no other discount claim is made.
- **AC-06** — Entitlement contract. (a) Every served catalogue entry has `availability` (all `null`) and `availability_domain`, and a `crm_entitlement` object. (b) `plan: "free"` returns no catalogue entry. (c) `crm_plan: "free"` returns only entries whose `crm_entitlement.free` is `true` or `"on_request"` and lists served entries whose `free` is `null` in `entitlement_unconfirmed` with a matching count. (d) No entry is reported available on a CRM plan unless its decision record says so.
- **AC-07** — NEGATIVE: `npm run check:served` exits non-zero when any of these is planted in a scratch copy — a banned phrase outside the exceptions list; a missing `limitations`/`availability_note`/`crm_entitlement`/`availability`; a malformed, missing or future `verified_at`/`decided_on`; a `pending`, `excluded` or revoked record that is served; `master-only` scope on an `approved` record; a served key outside the allowlist; an over-size response; a stale known-exception — and exits zero on the real data.
- **AC-08** — `README.md` lists `industries_list_modules` and `industries_get_modules`, and its stated tool counts equal `/version` `tools_total` on the candidate.
- **AC-09** — Gate tests for **every query path**, for both tool families and the aliases: with a record `pending`, `excluded` or revoked it is absent from `features_list` (every category, `search` matching its title, summary or alias, `plan`, `crm_plan`), `features_get` (by slug and by alias; same error text as an unknown slug), `industries_list_modules` (every `kind`, `search`), `industries_get_modules` (by key, name and alias) and from `entitlement_unconfirmed`; **and no served industry text names the title or alias of a `pending` catalogue entry** (leak test), so approving a vertical cannot publish an unapproved capability.

- **AC-11** — Q0, Q1 and Q3 are answered in writing and translated into per-record decisions before any of #95/#96/#97 is marked ready for review; undecided records remain `pending`.

## 8. Risks and edge cases
- **TEST ≠ PROD (Q0).** Code on `app` is not proof of a live feature (§1 row 6). The only gate is a recorded per-entry founder decision; entries default to `pending` and are not served.
- **Entitlement guesswork.** "Plan entitlement not confirmed" is shown as `null` tiers plus a note; it does not tell a buyer whether they can obtain the capability. The founder decision (`approved` with plan, or `on_request`) is what answers that, and an assistant may still drop the qualifier when repeating the entry — accepted, mitigated by putting the limit in the first line of `availability_note` where it exists.
- **Public repo and evidence.** The MCP repo may be public; internal product repo paths and approver names stay out of served data and out of this repo (AC-16), kept in the founder-held workbook.
- **The check proves less than the criterion title suggests.** `check:served` catches banned phrases, missing fields, pending-served, size and age. It cannot tell an availability claim from a limitation or catch a new kind of over-claim; each entry's wording is reviewed by the founder when deciding (Q1/Q3).
- **Staleness and invalidation.** The catalogue is hand-maintained. `verified_at` ages are checked at build **and weekly against the live server** (AC-13); a product change to a capability's flag, plan gate or migration invalidates its record (§0c). A decision can outlive a CRM rollback until the age rule fires — accepted, bounded to 90 days. Generation from the inventory is registered (R-a).
- **Locale and currency.** Local-currency prices (GBP, CAD, AUD, AED, SGD, NZD) come from the 2026-08-06 mirror; USD annual ($10) is founder-unconfirmed. `market_scope` states only what the founder confirmed (India 899 + GST; USD 12 monthly; a yearly discount exists, evidenced per AC-05) and no USD annual figure; the annual USD cell served by the plans remains a Q2 known-exception, not certified.
- **Alias confusion.** 16 industry aliases resolve to another vertical's screens (`reuses`); a vertical with no screens says "configuration only".
- **Payload size and abuse.** Character ceilings (AC-12) are a proxy, not a token budget; the existing rate limiter applies; paging is #81's.
- **Enum consumers.** Eight new `category` values can break a client that exhaustively handles the old seven; recorded in the CHANGELOG (AC-14).
- **Assistant answer quality is unmeasured.** Nothing here proves assistants recommend HelloGrowthCRM more; a before/after prompt test is registered, not built.
- **Rate limits, cost:** no new outbound calls; static data only.
